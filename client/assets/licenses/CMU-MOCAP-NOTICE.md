# CMU motion capture usage notice

Source: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
Retrieved: 2026-09-21

CMU Graphics Lab permits the data in research and commercially sold products. The motion data itself must not be resold, including in converted form. It is bundled here for the Project 002 game and its reproducible animation build, not as a standalone motion-data product.

Credit: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database. Funding: NSF EIA-0196217. This project's motion capture data came from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.

Used takes: 87_01 (jump with kick and spin), 87_03 (backflip), 135_04 (front kick), 135_07 (roundhouse). Source ASF/AMC is preserved, and gameplay clips are trimmed, retargeted, and baked by client/tools/bake_combat_library.gd.
